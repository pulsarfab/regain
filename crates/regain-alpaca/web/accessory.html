Wheel calibration

Rotate the wheel to detect all slots. Takes about 50 seconds and finishes at slot 1. Wait for completion before disconnecting; the wheel has no halt command.

Filters

Slots are shown as 1–N here. ASCOM uses 0–N−1. Keep at least one focus offset at zero.

Focuser settings

Check mechanical clearance before moving. Use zero hardware backlash if NINA handles compensation. Microns per step depend on the attached focuser; temperature compensation is managed by the imaging application.
